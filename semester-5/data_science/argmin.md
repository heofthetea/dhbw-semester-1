> the _argument_ of a [[Abbildung|Function]] that yields an [[lokales Extremum|Extremum]]

Say $f: \mathbb{R} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ is a function with a minimum at $\begin{bmatrix}0 \\ 1\end{bmatrix}$ 

Then:
- $\min f = 1$ (as it is part of the [[Bild einer Funktion|image]])
- $\arg \min f = 0$ (as it is part of the [[Urbild|pre-image]]) 

## Notation
$$\operatorname*{arg\,min}_{x}\ f(x)$$
